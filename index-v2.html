Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common concerns from teachers

Practical, up-to-date guidance on teaching, visas, and living in Vietnam.

How does your Job Placement service work for foreign teachers?

We work directly with certified public schools, bilingual academies, and language centers across Hanoi, Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City, and provincial cities. Based on your qualifications (Bachelor's degree, TEFL/CELTA, nationality), we connect you with suitable schools, schedule interviews, and assist with offer negotiation.

What is COE Support and why is it crucial for Filipino teachers?

For Filipino teachers departing the Philippines to work in Vietnam, immigration protocols require specific employment documentation (Certificate of Employment - COE, contract verification, and departure clearance). Without proper verification, teachers face high risks of offloading at Philippine airports. Our specialized desk coordinates with your hiring school to ensure all documents meet strict requirements for a hassle-free departure.

Can I convert a Tourist Visa or E-visa to a Business (DN1) or Work Visa?

Under Vietnamese immigration regulations, status transitions depend on having a legitimate sponsoring organization (your hiring school or educational company). We guide you on whether an in-country transition is possible for your specific passport and entry stamp, or assist with a smooth border run or new entry letter when required.

How long does Consular Legalization take? Is there an urgent option?

Yes, we offer both Standard and Fast-Track options. Standard consular authentication and certified judicial translation typically takes 7 to 10 working days, while our fast-track express option can expedite processing depending on embassy workload and document origin.

Can you assist with accommodation and airport pick-up when I arrive?

Absolutely. Through our trusted partner network of local real estate agents and transport providers, we can arrange safe private airport pickup straight to your accommodation, as well as preview apartment rentals situated near your teaching campus.
